# Design context: one_piece dashboard

- **Audience:** the researcher who launched a run (first user: a university student running swarms in the background between classes). Reads it asynchronously, often after hours away, sometimes live.
- **Jobs:** see the swarm's shape and state at a glance; open any agent and understand what it is doing and why; select a family and see how it is doing; follow token spend by branch, family, role and provider; read family boards; follow epochs and handoffs; answer questions escalated to them; read reports with their evidence.
- **Tone:** a precise scientific instrument. Calm, legible, information-dense without clutter. Warm paper-and-ink neutrals, a few meaningful status colours, no glow, no gradients, no AI-dashboard clichés.
- **Must:** circular nodes in a tree graph; clicking a node opens a side panel; families are selectable; every number is easy to read and compare.
